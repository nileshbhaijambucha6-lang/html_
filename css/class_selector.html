the haunted house

One dark night, Rahul was walking home when he saw an old house surrounded by dead trees. Suddenly, he heard a strange voice calling his name. He slowly entered the house and saw a shadow moving across the wall. The doors slammed shut, and a cold wind touched his face. Suddenly, a ghost appeared with glowing red eyes and a terrifying smile. Rahul screamed and ran toward the door, but it would not open. The ghost whispered, “You are mine now!” The next morning, villagers found the house empty, but Rahul’s screams could still be heard at midnight.

the birth of lord ganesha

### 🙏 The Birth of Lord Ganesha Lord Ganesha was born to Goddess Parvati and Lord Shiva. One day, Goddess Parvati created a boy from the sandalwood paste on her body and gave him life. She asked him to guard the entrance while she bathed. When Lord Shiva returned, the boy stopped him from entering. Angered by this, Lord Shiva cut off the boy’s head. Goddess Parvati was heartbroken and demanded that her son be brought back to life. To comfort her, Lord Shiva ordered his followers to bring the head of the first living being they found. They returned with an elephant’s head. Lord Shiva placed it on the boy’s body, revived him, and named him Lord Ganesha, the remover of obstacles and the god of wisdom and prosperity. 🙏

the ghost in the mirror

One night, a girl named Riya was alone at home. At midnight, she heard someone knocking on her bedroom door, but nobody was there. Suddenly, she noticed a strange woman standing in her mirror. Riya turned around, but the room was empty. When she looked back at the mirror, the woman was smiling and slowly raising her hand. Riya ran toward the door, but it locked by itself. Then, a cold voice whispered, “You should not have looked at me.” The mirror cracked, and a pale hand reached out. The next morning, her room was empty. Only the mirror remained, showing Riya smiling inside it. 👻
