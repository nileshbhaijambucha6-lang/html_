Gujarat is a beautiful state in western India. Its capital is Gandhinagar. Ahmedabad is the largest city in Gujarat. Gujarat is famous for its rich culture, traditions, and festivals. Navratri is one of its most popular festivals. The state is known for delicious Gujarati food, such as dhokla, fafda, and thepla. The Statue of Unity is a famous tourist attraction. Gujarat is also known for the Gir National Park, where Asiatic lions live. Mahatma Gandhi was born in Porbandar, Gujarat. The people of Gujarat are friendly and hardworking. I am proud of my state, Gujarat.

Jammu and Kashmir is a beautiful region of India. It is famous for its snow-covered mountains, green valleys, and beautiful lakes. Srinagar is a popular tourist destination. Dal Lake is one of its most famous attractions. Jammu and Kashmir is also known as the Paradise on Earth because of its natural beauty. People visit this region to enjoy snowfall, houseboats, and gardens. Apples, saffron, and dry fruits are famous products of this region. The people have a rich culture and traditions. Jammu and Kashmir is one of the most beautiful places in India.
